# Reported result summaries

These small CSV files record the principal numerical results reported in the revised manuscript. They are included for transparency and convenient checking; the notebooks remain the computational source of the analyses.

- `independent_validation_summary.csv`: abstention-aware independent evaluation.
- `nominal_contextual_composition.csv`: forest composition at the nominal hard-label 500 m / 0.75 definition.
- `parameter_sensitivity_summary.csv`: fraction of parameter-sensitive pixels for the hard-label and probability-context formulations.
- `scale_matched_comparison_summary.csv`: final matched-support agreement and mixed-forest proportions.

Global land-cover products are comparison products, **not ground truth**, so their values are described as agreement rather than validation accuracy.
